12. THE TELEPHONE DEATH

By Abraham Lincoln Bell

"Aleck, why didn't you answer my text...?"

Once upon a time, Aleck dialed a serial telephone number for his friend Aldrich. But Aldrich denied in favor of texting. Hours later, Aldrich was caught in a tragic fire accident.

For endless hours, Aldrich texted Aleck for help. But Aleck denied replying out of guilt and protocol fear. Aldrich perished in the flames.

Moral Lesson: "Sometimes when there is trouble we must receive a protocol/text don't deny so there's no mistrust."
